# Product Vision

## What CFOXPERT is

CFOXPERT is a **Business Performance & Enterprise Value Creation Platform**, not an accounting, bookkeeping, or tax firm. Virtual CFO services are the delivery mechanism; the product is a system that continuously assesses, scores, monitors, and improves a client's Enterprise Value.

## The Six Drivers (Enterprise Value Engine)

Every recommendation, dashboard, KPI, and monthly review maps to one or more of:
1. Financial Strength
2. Operational Excellence
3. Strategic Growth
4. Governance & Leadership
5. Technology & Intelligence
6. Capital & Valuation

## The Client Journey

```
Health Check → Health Score → Interactive Dashboard → Monthly KPI Tracking
   → Board Packs → Action Tracker → Document Vault → AI Insights
   → Benchmark Reports → Client Portal → (repeat, continuously)
```

This is a loop, not a funnel. The platform's job is to keep clients moving through it every month, not to deliver a one-time report.

## Core Product Principle

Never build isolated pages or isolated features. Every module is reusable and integrates with every other module. The long-term shape is one seamless Business Operating System — not a collection of tools that happen to share a login.

## Non-Goals (current)

- Not a bank-feed aggregator (accounting/ERP integrations are planned, last in the build order, and always feed the KPI Engine — they are not a separate product surface).
- Not a fully autonomous AI decision-maker. AI Services (Milestone 13) are assistive and human-reviewed before anything reaches a client.
- Not multi-currency/multi-country tax-aware in the near term — INR/India is the initial market; the schema reserves room for this (see `Database Schema.md`) without building it now.
